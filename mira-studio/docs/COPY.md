## ABOUT

**H1:** "A studio built around your week, not ours."
*(Concept fiction — the studio described here is invented for this sample.)*

Story: "Mira began as a single chair and one question: what if a studio kept time for the things that take time? A colour that develops properly. A cut that grows out well. Advice you can keep.

The answer became two rooms: a light one for hair, a quieter one for skin. No hard sell, no scent bomb, no 'best in the city' — just a menu with honest prices and appointments with room in them.

You'll always know what you're paying, roughly how long it will take, and who's doing it. The rest is hair, skin, and a little time for yourself."

**Values (3)**
1. **Time over turnover** — "Longer slots, fewer chairs, no double-booking."
2. **Advice you can keep** — "If the honest answer is 'less than you think', you'll hear that."
3. **No noise** — "No badges, no shouting; just the work."

**The space** — "Two rooms, full of daylight: one full of mirrors, one full of quiet. Coffee while colour develops."
